SEXC (Single Extruder X Carriage)
 SEXC was born from the creation of the SDEXC. As I was trying to figure out an easy way to have two hotends with easily adjustable heights while still being hot-swapable, I discovered that half of and SDEXC make a really nice, lightweight single extruder x carriage assembly! The toolhead mounts to the carriage plate via a friction fit dovetail groove. This allows for the easiest maintenance on any x carriage assembly currently available!
 I became obsessed and iterated over and over. I eventually designed my own extruder housing inspired by the BMG geared extruder, and started removing any unneeded material. It is nearly 1/3 of the weight of a BMG housing! The gears are my custom design, are printed via SLM (Selective Laser Melting) and are made of Cobalt-Chromium, an alloy harder than traditional brass or stainless steel gears. This model will soon be for sale! For the future, I am working on another protype with an even lighter stepper motor and extrduer housing, printed out of the lightest manmade polymer, Polypropylene, with carbon fiber. This model is expected to way less than a comparable Bambu Labs print head.
